CHANAKYA PORTAL - COMPREHENSIVE BUSINESS WORKFLOW & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================

1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & KEY ROLES
------------------------------
The Chanakya Portal is an internal vendor and expense management tool. 
- Front-end: Microsoft Power Apps
- Back-end/Database: SharePoint
- Logic/Workflows: Microsoft Power Automate

Key Roles (Who Does What):
* Real Estate Developer (Client): Provides the master budget for a given project (e.g., Kamdhinu developer providing ₹50 Lakhs).
* Strategy Team / SPOC: The primary decision-makers for a project. They receive the total budget, bifurcate it among different Verticals (e.g., Digital, Marketing), and approve major vendors and financial requests.
* Vertical Head / POC: The people executing the marketing/sales work (e.g., Digital Marketing Head). They onboard new vendors, request expenses, and consume the budget to hit their goals.
* CPT (Central Procurement Team): Resolves systemic budget adjustments (like adding or shifting funds). Also kept in the loop (CC'ed) on onboarding.

2. DATABASE & FINANCIAL BUCKETS
-------------------------------
To understand how money flows, the system tracks expenses using four main "Buckets" for each combination of Project, Vertical, and Budget Head (Service):
[Budget]            -> The total limit assigned to a Vertical/Service by Strategy.
[Available Balance] -> The actual cash currently available to be requested. Drops when new expenses are approved.
[Approved Amount]   -> Money reserved/pre-approved for a specific timeframe (e.g., monthly) but not entirely spent yet. Think of it like a gift card balance.
[Consumption]       -> The total money actually spent and burnt by the Vertical Head on daily tasks.


3. MODULE 1: VENDOR ONBOARDING
------------------------------
* Purpose: To add a new Vendor into the system (No pricing is discussed yet).
* Business Flow:
  1. The Vertical Head (Requester) enters vendor details (PAN, Aadhaar, services provided) into the Power Apps form.
  2. The System validates the PAN and Aadhaar formats to ensure they are correct.
  3. The System automatically sends an acknowledgement email to the Vertical Head, CC'ing the Strategy SPOC and CPT informing them of the new vendor.
  4. CPT will approve the vendor.

4. MODULE 2: VENDOR EVALUATION
------------------------------
* Purpose: To select and finalize 1 Vendor out of at least 3 onboarded vendors for a specific service based on price and efficiency.
* Business Flow:
  1. The Vertical Head (Requester) selects 3 onboarded vendors for a specific vertical/budget head. They input the vendors' pricing (commercials), service details, and specify their "Preferred Vendor" with reasons.
  2. The System generates an Evaluation Request ID (e.g., REQ-01) and sets the initial status to 'In Progress'.
  3. The System identifies the Strategy SPOC for that specific project and sends them an Approval Request via Teams/Email.
  4. The Strategy SPOC evaluates the 3 options (Vendor 1, 2, or 3) and selects the best one based on cost-effectiveness and past performance (or they can 'Reject All').
  5. If Approved: The chosen vendor is added to the 'Evaluated Vendors' list. The Vertical Head receives an approval email (CC: Strategy, CPT). 
  6. If Rejected: The request status is updated to 'Rejected' and the Vertical Head is notified.


5. MODULE 3: EXPENSE REQUISITION
--------------------------------
* Purpose: To actually spend money using the finalized "Evaluated Vendor".
* Security Feature (The Double-Check): To prevent a negative balance (e.g., two people requesting the last ₹5 Lakhs at the same time), the system checks the Available Balance ONCE before sending the approval email, and a SECOND time right after the Strategy SPOC clicks "Approve". If the balance dropped in the meantime, the system Auto-Rejects the request.

Business Flow by Spending Type:
  A. Monthly (Pre-Approved / Booking)
     - Who: Vertical Head requests X amount for Y days.
     - Action: Sent to Strategy SPOC for approval.
     - Flow: If approved, X is deducted from [Available Balance] and added to the [Approved Amount]. [Consumption] remains zero.
  
  B. Daily (Spending from the Booking)
     - Who: Vertical Head spends Y amount per day from their already pre-approved monthly bucket.
     - Action: NO Strategy approval is required, as long as it is within the [Approved Amount] limit.
     - Flow: Y is deducted from [Approved Amount] and added to [Consumption]. [Available Balance] does not change.
  
  C. Direct (One-off Immediate Spends)
     - Who: Vertical Head requests Z amount immediately for a quick, unbooked service.
     - Action: Sent to Strategy SPOC for approval.
     - Flow: If approved, Z is deducted directly from [Available Balance] and added directly to [Consumption]. [Approved Amount] is bypassed entirely.
Money left in available balance by the end of the month will be addded to next month's budget, (in the new app)

6. MODULE 4: BUDGET ADDITION & SHIFTING
---------------------------------------
* Purpose: To modify the project's financial buckets after the initial budget was set.
* Rules: Money can ONLY be shifted within the same Project. You cannot move funds between different developers' projects.

Business Flow by Type:
  A. Budget Addition
     - Scenario: The Developer injects more cash into the project.
     - Who: CPT (Central Procurement Team) logs the request.
     - Action: Sends to Strategy SPOC for approval.
     - Flow: No auto-rejections occur. Once approved, the new money is added to both the Total [Budget] and the [Available Balance] columns.
     
  B. Budget Shifting
     - Scenario: One department has excess funds, and another is running out.
     - Who: CPT selects a 'From' budget head (giver) and a 'To' budget head (receiver) within the same project.
     - Action: The System checks if the 'From' bucket has enough [Available Balance] to give away. If it does, approval is sent to Strategy SPOC.
     - Flow: If approved, the amount is subtracted from the giver's [Budget] and [Available Balance], and added to the receiver's [Budget] and [Available Balance].
