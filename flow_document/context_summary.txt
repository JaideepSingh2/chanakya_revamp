# Chanakya Portal Revamp: Context & Prompt Summary

## Project Context
The project involves creating a comprehensive flow document for a new Model-Driven Power App called **Chanakya Portal**. The app is designed for internal vendor and expense management at a real estate development firm.

### Key Actors & Roles:
- **Strategy SPOC:** Decision-maker, approves financial requests.
- **Vertical POC:** Executes operations (marketing/sales), onboards vendors, requests expenses.
- **CPT (Central Procurement Team):** Manages structural budget shifts and vendor onboarding approvals.
- **Tech Admin:** Initializes the system with master budget data.

### Core Modules:
1. **Module 0: Setup** - Budget upload from SharePoint/Excel to Dataverse.
2. **Module 1: Vendor Onboarding** - Adding new vendors to the system.
3. **Module 2: Vendor Evaluation** - Comparing 3 vendors and selecting a preferred one.
4. **Module 3: Expense Requisition** - Spending against the budget (Monthly, Daily, and Direct paths).
5. **Module 4: Budget Operations** - Shifting funds or adding new capital.
